# Methodology Notes

Some of the highest-quality job-search methodology in print comes from the LDS Church's *Find a Better Job* self-reliance course. The methods stand alone for anyone — religious or not. Take what's useful, skip what isn't.

---

## 15-10-2 — the daily numbers

Every day:
- **15** new leads identified
- **10** contacts made (applications, resume sends, thank-yous, DMs, calls — anything that touches another human or their inbox)
- **2** face-to-face meetings (video counts; coffee is better)

The "2 face-to-face" is the keystone. Most job seekers underestimate how rare and how powerful real conversations are.

[Method writeup](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/using-the-15-10-2-method-in-your-job-search?lang=eng) · [Full course manual](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/find-a-better-job-for-self-reliance?lang=eng)

## Me in 30 Seconds — the personal pitch

Four parts, ~75-90 words total:

1. **Who you are + personal connection** (10-15 words)
2. **What you're looking for** (10-15 words)
3. **Strengths + one proof point** (25-30 words)
4. **Call to action — a question they can answer** (15-20 words)

Use this in every hallway conversation, every Zoom intro, every airport conversation. Memorize it.

---

## Power Statements — interview answer scaffolding

Short, evidence-based answers that pair a skill with a quantified result. Each Power Statement:
- Names the skill
- Gives a concrete situation where you used it
- Names the action you took
- Ends with a quantified outcome

This is essentially STAR + Q. Use these as the building blocks for behavioral interview prep.

---

## Action partner — the daily accountability mechanic

The single most underused tactic in any job search. Pair with someone — a friend, a sibling, a former coworker also searching, a mentor — who you text every evening:

> "I committed to X today, and I [did it / didn't and here's why]. Tomorrow I'll commit to Y."

Two weeks of this changes momentum more than any tool. The mechanic isn't about willpower — it's about turning "I should do this" into "I told someone I would."

In the *Find a Better Job* course this is built in: people pair during the weekly meeting and check in daily between meetings. You don't need the course to use the mechanic.

---

## Weekly written commitments

Physically write down next week's actions and have someone else co-sign them. Trivial; works. The co-signing matters — accountability needs another set of eyes on it.

---

## Employment Resource Services (ERS) — free coaching, open to everyone

The LDS Church runs **Employment Resource Services**, which is open to non-members. Free resume help, mock interviews, coaching, and a real network of employers who hire through Church channels. Walk-in or scheduled.

- Salt Lake City center: 724 E 2100 S
- Smaller centers in major metros
- Remote/phone coaching available

Use it like a free career-services office at a university. The coaches are pros, the cost is zero, no faith ask attached to the service.

[Overview article](https://www.thechurchnews.com/2022/2/25/23216701/church-employment-services-helps-unemployed-people-find-jobs-self-reliance-connection-to-savior/)
