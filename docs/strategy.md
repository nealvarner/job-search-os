# The Strategy

Distilled from Aakash Gupta, Justin Welsh, Steve Dalton (*The 2-Hour Job Search*), Austin Belcak (*Cultivated Culture*), and the LDS Church's *Find a Better Job* course. Read once end-to-end; refer back to §5 (Daily Cadence) as the operating doc.

---

## 1. Three things to internalize before anything else

1. **Applying online is fishing in a pond with 500 other lines in it.** ~60-70% of hires come from referrals, internal moves, and 2nd-degree network — *before* the role hits public job boards. If you spend 80% of your time on the 30-40% of jobs that are publicly posted, you've inverted the math.
2. **Talking to people is the job search.** The LDS *Find a Better Job* course teaches **15-10-2** daily: 15 new leads, 10 contacts, 2 face-to-face meetings. Dalton independently lands on the same number. This is the work.
3. **Specificity wins every battle.** Specific headline > generic. Specific outreach > generic. Specific company list > "I'll take anything."

## 3. Dalton's 2-Hour Job Search

Steve Dalton compresses the strategy work into ~2 hours up front:

- **LAMP list (~70 min):** 40 target employers, scored 1-3 on:
  - **L** — list (brain-dump candidates)
  - **A** — Advocacy: do you know anyone there?
  - **M** — Motivation: do you actually want to work there?
  - **P** — Posting: do they have a relevant opening or hire your role?
  - Sort by total. Work top-down.
- **Contact (~30 min):** One alum or 2nd-degree connection per company. Send the **6-point email** (template in [outreach-templates.md §3](outreach-templates.md)).
- **3B7 follow-up rule:** Reminder at **3 business days** to move to the next contact; one polite follow-up at **7 days** to the original. Never more.
- **TIARA in the call:** Trends → Insights → Advice → Resources → Assignments. Start treating them as expert, end treating them as mentor. ([outreach-templates.md §11](outreach-templates.md))

## 4. The Belcak overlay — "do the work first"

For your top 5-10 dream companies, build a **Value Validation Project**: a 1-3 page deliverable that solves a real problem they have, sent unsolicited.

For an engineer: a code teardown, a perf audit, a "how I'd solve [X] given your stack." For a PM: a strategy memo, a product critique with 3 prioritized fixes. For a designer: a UX teardown. For a TA pro: a sourcing audit, a candidate-experience walkthrough, a time-to-fill benchmark.

Send the link with:
> *"Made this for fun. No reply needed — just wanted you to have it."*

Response rates jump from ~5% to 30-50% because (a) it proves you can do the job, (b) it triggers reciprocity, (c) it's memorable in a sea of "love your work!"

Use sparingly — once per target company, for roles you genuinely want. Pair with the cold email a week later.

## 5. Daily / Weekly cadence — the 15-10-2

Print this. Tape it above your desk.

### Daily (90-120 min)

| Block | Action |
|---|---|
| Morning (60 min) | **15 new leads** added to LAMP list, scored, top 5 selected for today's outreach |
| | **5 cold outreaches** (Dalton 6-point email or LinkedIn DM). One must be a hiring manager at a top-LAMP company. |
| | **3 second-degree intro requests** |
| | **2 follow-ups** per the 3B7 rule |
| Midday (30 min) | **LinkedIn presence:** 5 substantive comments on hiring-manager / leader posts. 1 post Mon/Wed/Fri. |
| Afternoon (30 min) | **Apply to 3-5 high-fit roles** with Tailor-customized resume. Stop at 5. |
| Evening (60 min) | **2 face-to-face conversations** (Zoom counts). TIARA framework. Thank-you note within 24h. |

### Weekly

- **Monday morning:** review LAMP, remove dead leads, set 3 goals.
- **Friday afternoon:** update pipeline, write 1 win + 1 lesson, send 3 staying-warm messages to people from 2-4 weeks ago.
- **Sunday:** rest.

### 90-day arc

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Profile rewrite. LAMP list built. Me-on-a-Page drafted. 50-person network blast sent. |
| Weeks 2-4 | Full 15-10-2 cadence. First wave of informational interviews. First LinkedIn posts shipped. |
| Weeks 5-8 | Referral pipeline kicks in. Belcak VVPs sent to top 5 dream companies. First-round interviews. |
| Weeks 9-12 | Final rounds, offers, negotiation. Target: 3-5 offers in flight by week 12. |

## 6. The LinkedIn profile

Recruiter search in 2026 is **semantic**, not keyword-match. Vary phrasing across sections; don't repeat keywords.

### Headline (220 chars max — biggest ranking signal)

Formula: `[Target role + level] | [Specialization] | [Top company or big metric] | [Geo / availability]`

### About (2,200 char max — write in 1st person)

```
HOOK (1 sentence — what you do and your edge)
↓
2-3 STAR-format wins (quantified)
↓
Specialization keyword block (mined from 5 target JDs)
↓
What you're looking for + CTA (email or LinkedIn DM)
```

### Other rules

- **Featured section:** cap at 2 items. More than 2 get cropped to half-width.
- **Skills:** pin the 3 most-searched skills for your target role. Mine from real JDs.
- **Recommendations:** target 5+. Send drafts in your asker's voice so it's nearly impossible to say no.
- **Banner:** 1584×396px. Single value-prop sentence + how to reach you. Not a stock photo.
- **"Open to Work":** use **recruiters-only** setting, not the green ring (which leaks to coworkers and signals desperation to hiring managers).
- **Activity:** 1 post/week + 5 substantive comments/day. The 210-char hook rule — only ~210 chars show before "see more"; engineer line 1 to break scroll.

## 7. ATS / Resume — top 7 rules

1. **Single column. No tables, text boxes, icons, skill bars, headshots.**
2. **Submit as .docx** (parses better than PDF in 2026 Workday/Greenhouse). Never image-PDF.
3. **Match 3-5 exact noun phrases from the JD.** If they wrote "cross-functional collaboration," write that — not "team coordination."
4. **Quantify ≥60% of bullets.**
5. **Spell out acronyms once.**
6. **Standard section headers** ("Work Experience," "Education," "Skills").
7. **Test parse:** paste into Notepad. If sections scramble, the ATS sees that too.

---

## 8. What to avoid (hard rules)

1. **No spray-and-pray applications.** 10 targeted outreaches > 100 cold apps.
2. **Don't say "I'd love to pick your brain."** Dead phrase. Say "15-minute call" with a specific topic.
3. **Don't ask for a job in an informational interview.** Ask for advice; people offer jobs.
4. **Never attach a resume on first contact.** Link to LinkedIn.
5. **Don't paste your resume into your About section.** About is 1st-person narrative.
6. **Don't use the green Open-to-Work ring** if employed. Recruiters-only setting.
7. **Don't stack >2 items in Featured.**
8. **Don't keyword-stuff.** 2026 ATS + LinkedIn are semantic.
9. **Don't follow up >3x without new info.**
10. **Don't skip the thank-you** within 24h of an informational interview.
11. **Don't use mass-apply tools** (LazyApply, Sonara unsupervised). They torch your reputation.
12. **Don't use live interview AI copilots.** Detection is rising; if caught, you're not hireable in that industry.
